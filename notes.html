Clinical notes

🔒 Stays on this iPad only. Nothing is uploaded and nothing goes into the app’s code. The note is wiped when you tap Start new note, and an unfinished note is wiped by itself after 3 days. Save the PDF to Clever Clinic before you start the next one.

Patient and visit

Dictate the whole note

Tap Dictate and speak the whole note. Say each heading, then pause, and the words sort themselves into the right section when you tap Stop. Each sentence becomes a bullet point.

  • Headings: Treatment decision · Procedure (or “Procedure: Restylane skin boosters”) · Aftercare · Skin consultation · Skincare routine · Paid partnership · Plan and next visit
  • Fields: “Product: …” · “Areas treated: …” · “Next appointment: …”
  • Say “next point” to start a new bullet.

Clinical photos

For the clinical record only, never for marketing. Photos stay on this iPad and are printed at the end of the PDF.

Sign

Your name, role, registration number and signature are saved on this iPad for next time. They are not patient details.

PDF

Tap Save or send PDF and choose Save to Files, then upload it to the patient’s record in Clever Clinic.
